# bill's game board — Three-Game Scenic Art Spec

Status: LOCKED FOR CURRENT DESIGN DIRECTION
Date locked: 2026-08-17
Scope: Background/scenic framing art only. This lock may be revised later without changing gameplay architecture.

## Global scenic-art rule

The game scenes are illustrated framing art, not literal live puzzle boards.

The intended look is stylized scenic illustration: polished, dimensional, atmospheric, believable without being photorealistic, and richer than flat clip art. Foreground details should be clearest; background environments should soften and recede so they support mood rather than compete with the game.

Bill remains the app's host/brand anchor but is not required inside every game-specific scenic image.

The scenic art may imply that a puzzle is already being worked on through pencils, eraser marks, candidate notes, crossed-off words, partially completed answers, or similar details. These marks belong to the artwork only. The actual interactive puzzle UI must remain visually distinct, clean, legible, and easy to operate.

Different games do not need to share one historical era. Each game should use the scenic metaphor that best suits it.

## Crossword — Cozy Den Edition

### Core concept
A cozy, intimate puzzle moment: a partially completed crossword rests on the lap of a seated person relaxing in a warm den.

### View and composition
- Viewpoint angled toward the player's lap.
- Crossword page rests across bent knees or crossed legs.
- Puzzle/newspaper is the foreground focal point.
- The composition should feel naturally in-progress rather than staged as a product shot.

### Foreground details
- Folded newspaper or puzzle page with an original fictional newspaper identity if visible.
- Several crossword answers already filled in.
- Pencil nearby or in hand.
- Subtle eraser marks, penciled corrections, and normal signs of use.
- Optional lightly worked clue area.

### Human-context details
- Slippers visible.
- Pajamas visible.
- Robe draped loosely over the knee, naturally slipping or falling partly away from it.
- Full person does not need to be shown.

### Background environment
- Cozy wood-paneled den.
- Fireplace.
- Mantel.
- Warm seating / reading-room atmosphere.
- Background softly recedes and remains secondary to the newspaper and lap.

### Mood
Warm, quiet, cozy, lived-in, relaxed morning-or-evening puzzle time.

### Bill usage
No Bill mascot required in the scenic composition.

## Word Search — Nostalgic Puzzle-Book Edition

### Core concept
A nostalgic word-search activity-book scene inspired by the familiar large-format puzzle books people buy for casual solving, while using completely original branding and cover design.

### View and composition
- Angled tabletop or lap view.
- The puzzle book/page is the central object and should be easy to recognize.
- A stronger tilt is acceptable if it shows more of the page and surrounding props.

### Foreground details
- Oversized word-search book or booklet.
- A few words already circled, marked, or highlighted.
- Several completed words crossed off in the answer list.
- Pen, pencil, or marker nearby.
- Lightly worn printed-page feel.
- Optional shiny older-style puzzle-book cover partly visible.

### Style direction
- Nostalgic puzzle-book feel rather than a forced 1920s/1930s treatment.
- Can lean toward mid-century, 1970s, 1980s, or other familiar print-era activity-book aesthetics when appropriate.
- Must remain original rather than imitate a specific publisher's visual identity.

### Background environment
Simple casual reading context: tabletop, side table, breakfast nook, or comfortable reading area. Background may be less specific than Crossword.

### Mood
Playful, nostalgic, approachable, casual, familiar.

### Bill usage
No Bill mascot required in the scenic composition.

## Sudoku — Modern Window Edition

### Core concept
A calm modern lifestyle puzzle moment: a Sudoku puzzle is being worked on while seated in a bright, stylish contemporary interior.

### View and composition
- Sudoku sheet, booklet, or pad rests on a lap or supported surface.
- Moderate angled perspective so the page is clearly visible.
- Foreground puzzle is in focus; room and exterior recede softly.

### Foreground details
- Visible Sudoku grid.
- Several penciled candidate notes in cell corners.
- A few normal handwritten marks or light eraser traces.
- Pencil nearby or in hand.
- Scene should imply active logical problem solving without decorative clutter.

### Human-context details
- Seated person may be implied rather than fully shown.
- Knees/lap may frame the puzzle.
- Contemporary casual-comfort clothing is appropriate.

### Background environment
- Modern / chic apartment or room.
- Large bright window.
- Sunlight entering the room.
- Softly suggested exterior: grass, blue sky, trees, fence, or similar calm view.
- Background should use shallow-depth / softened-focus treatment so it remains atmospheric rather than literal.

### Mood
Calm, bright, focused, fresh, thoughtful, uncluttered.

### Bill usage
No Bill mascot required in the scenic composition.

## Perspective rule

The scenic surfaces do not use one fixed angle. Use a mild-to-moderate drafting-table/tabletop perspective and adjust it per game. Paper-based games such as Crossword, Word Search, and Sudoku may use a stronger upward tilt when it helps the artwork display the puzzle surface and foreground details.

## Usability boundary

Scenic art is presentation only. It must never create ambiguity about what is interactive, reduce text/grid contrast, hide touch targets, or make the active puzzle harder to read. Gameplay clarity always outranks decorative detail.
